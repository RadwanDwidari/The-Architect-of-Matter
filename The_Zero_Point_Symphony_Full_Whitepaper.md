Part III: Weak Points Analysis and Open Questions
Like any system of high complexity, our model has points of tension that require attention in the implementation or presentation phase.

Critical Weak Points (Risks)
Risk	Description
1. Carbon Residue Management	In the petroleum + water vapor combination, if the temperature drops below a certain threshold, carbon can deposit as soot (coking), blocking the hydrogen "transpiration."
2. The 20 km/h Speed Paradox	At a rate of 100 tons/second, the system's mass grows exponentially. Maintaining such a low speed requires immense braking force, which could generate residual heat difficult to evacuate even with the help of Cesium.
3. Instability in the 13th Second	The 13 seconds OFF / 1 second ON ratio every 5 cycles creates a negative pressure shock (vacuum). There is a risk of "implosion of the island of stability" if the reignition in the 1st second is not synchronized with millimeter precision.
Open Questions for Future Research

What is the source of emission during the OFF second? If the machinery is OFF, where does the energy needed to keep the cooling system active come from?
